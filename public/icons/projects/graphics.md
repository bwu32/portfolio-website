# Multi-Org Visual Communications

Throughout my time involved in various college student organizations, I served as a primary visual communicator, translating cultural values and event logistics into digestible, high-impact digital graphics. Whether it was for **Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA)**, **Mid-Atlantic Union Vietnamese Student Associations (MAUVSA)**, **Kharis Campus Ministry (KCM)**, or **Bethel College Fellowship (BCF)**, I utilized a mix of "pro-sumer" tools like **Canva** and specialized software like **Paint.NET** to maintain consistent brand identities across different missions.

## The Design Philosophy: "Cultural Modernism"

My work across these orgs shared a common goal: taking traditional cultural elements and making them feel relevant to a modern, college-aged audience.

- **TASA (Taiwanese American Student Association):** Focused on nostalgic, food-centric visuals and community-building flyers. I utilized clean layouts to highlight the intersection of Taiwanese heritage and American student life.
- **MAUVSA & VSA (Vietnamese Student Associations):** Developed high-energy promotional materials for regional conferences and local chapter events. This required balancing the prestige of a large-scale union (MAUVSA) with the approachable, "family" vibe of individual VSA chapters.
- **BCF (Bethel College Fellowship):** Designed faith-based and community-outreach graphics that focused on warmth and inclusivity, often using softer palettes and approachable typography.

## Technical Execution & Workflow

I treated every "quick flyer" or "Instagram story" with the same attention to detail as a major merch launch.

- **Paint.NET Mastery:** While often overlooked, I pushed **Paint.NET** to its limits for heavy-duty raster editing, layering, and texture work before most students were using more expensive suites.
- **Canva for Speed:** Leveraged **Canva** for collaborative projects and rapid-turnaround social media assets, ensuring that even under tight deadlines, the typography and alignment remained professional.
- **Asset Diversity:** My portfolio across these orgs includes:
    - **Informational Infographics:** Explaining cultural holidays or event schedules.
    - **Event Flyers:** High-contrast designs for Night Markets, conferences, and general body meetings.
    - **Branded Templates:** Creating reusable assets so other board members could maintain visual consistency.

## Impact & Leadership

Being the "graphics person" for four different organizations required high-level **Time Management** and **Client Communication**. I learned how to:
- **Interpret a Vision:** Taking vague requests from board members and turning them into polished visuals.
- **Brand Switching:** Successfully jumping between the "fun/cute" aesthetic of a sticker design to the "professional/corporate" look of a regional conference proposal.
- **Community Engagement:** My graphics were often the first point of contact for new members, directly contributing to record-high event attendance and membership sign-ups.
