# Maryland Wushu Club Jacket

For the University of Maryland Wushu club, I designed a custom Raglan sports jacket that balances a "team-first" athletic look with traditional martial arts aesthetics. The goal was to create a garment that looked like official university varsity gear while clearly honoring the Chinese heritage of the sport.

## Visual Identity & Branding

The design utilizes a high-contrast **Black and Red** palette to align with University of Maryland colors, emphasizing a "sleek" and aggressive athletic feel.

- **The Front (The Seal):** I developed a new, circular Wushu logo as the centerpiece. To ground the sport in the local community, I surrounded the logo with a **shell motif**, a direct nod to the Maryland Terrapin. Beneath the seal, I integrated "Maryland Wushu" in clean Mandarin characters.
- **The Back (The Statement):** The back of the jacket is designed for high visibility during competition and travel. It features a larger, red version of the circular logo, overlaid with "Maryland" and "Wushu" in stylized, gold Mandarin characters.
- **Typography:** To give the jacket a classic sports-team vibe, I added "Terpwushu Club" at the bottom in a fluid, gold cursive font, mimicking the look of professional baseball or basketball varsity jackets.

## Customization & Personalization

A key feature of this design was the "pro-athlete" treatment for individual members.

- **Personalized Neckline:** Each jacket features the wearer's name in gold, positioned right below the neckline on the back. This level of personalization not only builds club pride but also ensures the gear feels like a bespoke piece of equipment.
- **Vector Precision:** I utilized **Adobe Illustrator** to ensure every character and flourish—especially the gold cursive—was perfectly vectorized for high-quality screen printing or embroidery, ensuring the fine lines of the Mandarin characters remained legible.

## Design Philosophy

This project allowed me to experiment with **Modern Athleticism vs. Cultural Heritage**. By mixing the Raglan sleeve silhouette (known for its ease of motion in sports) with gold and red accents (traditional colors of luck and power in Chinese culture), I created a uniform that represents the "Modern Martial Artist"—someone who is a high-level athlete and a cultural ambassador.
